# Privacy Policy for Med Tracker

Last updated: 30th of September 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Med Tracker ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information. The App is designed as a privacy-first medication adherence tool. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

## Data We Collect and Store

**All data you enter into the App is stored locally on your device only.** We do not have access to it, and it is never transmitted to our servers.

This locally stored data includes:

- Medication information (name, dosage, form, color, notes, active status)
- Schedules (times of day, dose amounts, active days)
- Dose logs (taken, skipped, or missed statuses, timestamps)
- App preferences (notification settings, theme choices)

## How We Use Your Information

Your medication and adherence data is used **solely** to provide the App's core functionality:

- Displaying your medication list and schedule
- Showing which doses are due or missed
- Calculating your adherence percentage and streak
- Sending local notifications to remind you to take your medication

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

**We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to any third party.**

The App does not use analytics services, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools. No data leaves your device.

## Data Retention

All data is stored on your device for as long as you choose to keep the App installed. If you uninstall the App, **all locally stored data is permanently deleted** and cannot be recovered by us.

## Drug Interaction Checker

The App includes a drug interaction checker feature. When you use this feature, the names of the medications you have entered are sent to the National Institutes of Health (NIH) RxNav API to retrieve publicly available interaction data.

- **No personal identifiers are sent** — only medication names.
- **No account information, device ID, or profile data is transmitted.**
- The results are informational only and are not medical advice.
- The App does not store the interaction results on any server.

## Notification Permissions

The App requests permission to send notifications. This is used exclusively to deliver medication reminders you have scheduled within the App. You can revoke this permission at any time through your device settings.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

## Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it:

- **Access**: All your data is visible within the App
- **Deletion**: Uninstalling the App deletes all data
- **Correction**: You can edit or delete any medication, schedule, or dose log at any time

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
